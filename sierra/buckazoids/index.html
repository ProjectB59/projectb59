Buckazoids

1986 → 1991 → 2025

Buckazoids began as the fictional currency of Space Quest. This page separates their 1986 introduction, the familiar Space Quest IV coin display from 1991, and the modern research trail that brought the artwork back into public discussion in 2025.

1986 · Origin

The name comes first

In the original Space Quest, Buckazoids are already the game world’s currency. That establishes the name and its function. The familiar gold coin artwork does not appear until later. The next step in the trail is Space Quest IV, where the coin graphic and the changing Buckazoid balance can be examined directly.

1991 · Space Quest IV

The familiar coin display

Space Quest IV uses the gold Buckazoid inventory graphic that later became the focus of modern comparisons and research.

Documented

59 is the starting balance

The inventory begins with a displayed balance of 59 Buckazoids.

Documented

Later gameplay shows 2,009

The same coin graphic later appears with a balance of 2,009. This demonstrates that the number is a changing inventory balance rather than a fixed denomination printed on the coin artwork.

2025 · Rediscovery

Why the old coin became relevant again

On 27 March 2025, a post titled “BTC in a game from 1991?” brought the Space Quest IV Buckazoid graphic into a new public discussion. That post is part of the modern rediscovery trail. It is not evidence that Sierra intended a connection to Bitcoin. From here, the research question becomes who created the final coin artwork and what can actually be established from surviving sources.

Research lead

“Cypherzoids” in the Cypherpunks archive

A preserved screenshot contains a Cypherpunks mailing-list message quoting Hal and a closing line by Paul Ferguson using the word “cypherzoids.” Project B59 preserves the screenshot and links the mailing-list archive used for the underlying source trail. The wordplay is not treated as evidence of a Sierra connection.

Open question

Who created the final coin artwork?

Mark Crowe has commented on the symbol and its intended money-like appearance, but the final artist attribution remains unresolved in the material currently assembled by Project B59.

Separate history

The modern BUCKAZOIDS token

The 2025 community token is a later project inspired by the rediscovered Sierra currency. Project B59 keeps the fictional currency history and the modern token history separate so neither is mistaken for the other.
