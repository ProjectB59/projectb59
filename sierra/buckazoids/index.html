Buckazoids

1986 → 1991 → 2025

Buckazoids began as the fictional currency of Space Quest. This research page separates their introduction in 1986, the familiar Space Quest IV coin display from 1991, and the modern research trail that brought the artwork back into public discussion in 2025.

1986

Buckazoids appear in Space Quest

Buckazoids already function as money in the original Space Quest. The currency predates the later gold coin artwork most people now associate with the name.

1991 · Space Quest IV

The familiar coin display

Space Quest IV uses the gold Buckazoid inventory graphic that later became the focus of modern comparisons and research.

Documented

59 is the starting balance

The inventory begins with a displayed balance of 59 Buckazoids.

Documented

Later gameplay shows 2,009

The same coin graphic later appears with a balance of 2,009. This demonstrates that the number is a changing inventory balance rather than a fixed denomination printed on the coin artwork.

Documented

The ATM payout is 2,001

Walkthrough material documents a 2,001-Buckazoid ATM withdrawal. A later balance of 2,009 means 2,001 should not be described as a maximum balance.

2025

Modern rediscovery

On 27 March 2025, a post titled “BTC in a game from 1991?” brought the Space Quest IV Buckazoid graphic into a new public discussion. Project B59 treats that post as part of the modern rediscovery trail, not as evidence that Sierra intended a connection to Bitcoin.

Research trail

What remains under investigation

Open question

Who created the final coin artwork?

Mark Crowe has commented on the symbol and its intended money-like appearance, but the final artist attribution remains unresolved in the material currently assembled by Project B59.

Separate history

The modern BUCKAZOIDS token

The 2025 community token is a later project inspired by the rediscovered Sierra currency. Project B59 keeps the fictional currency history and the modern token history separate so neither is mistaken for the other.

Research sources currently tracked by Project B59 include Space Quest gameplay captures, period walkthrough material, Space Quest reference archives, Sierra-related interviews, contemporary game coverage, and the 2025 rediscovery record.
