Sierra On-Line

1980 — 1997 · KEN & ROBERTA WILLIAMS ERA

Sierra On-Line helped define the personal-computer adventure era. This collection gathers Project B59’s Sierra research, including company history, Space Quest, Buckazoids, Sierra publications, developer interviews, SierraWeb records, and active research leads.

SierraWeb / Cypherpunks

Three surviving messages

These messages are a separate research finding within the Sierra collection. They document a SierraWeb account associated with the Cypherpunks mailing-list address. They are summarized here because they have not yet been accessioned as individual Vault records.

27 Aug 1995

Sierra On-Line FAQ

Automated SierraWeb account material was sent to cypherpunks@toad.com.

27 Aug 1995

Sierra On-Line Authentication Key

A matching automated message begins “Welcome cypherpunks to SierraWeb” and supplies an authentication / verification key.

13 Apr 1997

SierraWeb account message

A later Sierra On-Line message records a SierraWeb account using “cypherpunks” as both username and password.

Evidence boundary: these records establish that the account and automated messages existed. They do not identify who registered or used the account, and they do not establish a formal relationship between Sierra On-Line and the Cypherpunks.
