Sierra On-Line

1980 — 1997 · FOUNDING ERA

A growing research collection covering Sierra's early history, Space Quest, Buckazoids, company publications, developer interviews, SierraWeb records, and the source trails that connect them.

Why 1980–1997? The collection is centered on the company built by Ken and Roberta Williams. Sierra was acquired by CUC International in 1996, ending its independence; the transition continued through the following year as the Williams era closed.

Space Quest IV · 1991

The number on the Buckazoid display changes

Gameplay evidence resolves one recurring question about the Space Quest IV image.

Documented

59 is the starting balance

The familiar inventory display begins at 59 Buckazoids.

Documented

Later gameplay shows 2,009

The same coin graphic appears with a balance of 2,009. The changing value shows that the number is an inventory balance, not a fixed denomination engraved into the coin artwork.

Documented

The ATM pays 2,001

Walkthrough material documents a 2,001-Buckazoid ATM payout. Because a later balance reaches 2,009, 2,001 should not be described as the maximum possible balance.
