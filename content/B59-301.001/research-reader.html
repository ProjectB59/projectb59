DES Review at Stanford University -- NBS/NSA Meeting Transcript, 1976

Transcript of the historic 1976 meeting at Stanford University between Stanford computer science researchers, the National Bureau of Standards (NBS), and the National Security Agency (NSA) to discuss the proposed Data Encryption Standard (DES). This meeting was a pivotal moment in the Crypto Wars, where academic cryptographers challenged the government's encryption standard and the NSA's role in weakening it. Background notes by John Gilmore. Transcribed and published to preserve the historical record of government-academic conflict over public cryptographic standards.

John Gilmore (transcription), various speakers · 1976-01-01 · writing

Provenance
