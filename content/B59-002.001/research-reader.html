Cypherpunks Mailing List Archive -- Searchable by Contributor

Searchable archive of the Cypherpunks Mailing List organized by contributor, containing thousands of messages from key figures in cryptography and digital privacy. The archive indexes 28+ contributors including Timothy C. May (4,104 messages), Perry E. Metzger (2,042), Jim Bell (1,180), Eric Hughes (1,073), Duncan Frissell (991), and others down to single-digit contributions from figures like Bram Cohen and John Perry Barlow. Messages are filterable by label, subject, sender, and date. The Cypherpunks Mailing List was the primary forum where the technical and philosophical foundations of digital cash, anonymous communication, and cryptographic freedom were debated from 1992 onward.

Maaria Bajwa (archivist) · 1992-01-01 · website
