Reverse Engineering the Sega Channel Game Image File Format

Technical preservation record for an investigation into the Sega Channel broadcast image format. Using a recovered September 1996 Sega Channel game-image CD and earlier Foley Hi-Tech development materials, the author reverse engineered the tools that assembled and encoded the broadcast data, wrote compatible decoding code, and recovered playable menu, manual, exclusive, and prototype content.
