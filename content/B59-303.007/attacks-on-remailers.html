REMAIL: Attacks on Remailers

Eric Hughes · 1993-08-31
The whole point of a remailer network is that you have to subvert many of them in order to breach the security of the system.

The cypherpunk remailers of 1993 were simple: strip headers, decrypt one layer, forward. This thread is where the list worked out how weak that was against an observer who could watch traffic, and what would have to be added, delay, padding to a fixed size, batching and reordering, to make chaining actually resist analysis. Lance Cottrell's Mixmaster, released the following year, is essentially an implementation of the conclusions.
