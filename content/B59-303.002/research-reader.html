The Hopping Remailer Is Done -- Eric Hughes

Email from Eric Hughes to the Cypherpunks Mailing List announcing the completion of the first hopping remailer implementation, written in Perl. The system enabled sequential message forwarding through multiple remailers by parsing a 'Request-Remailing-To' header field. Hughes demonstrated the system successfully forwarding a test message through three sequential hops. He acknowledged the system was 'not at all secure by itself' but proposed that combining message body encryption with the hopping mechanism could conceal routing instructions. This is one of the earliest posts on the Cypherpunks Mailing List and documents the first practical implementation of chained anonymous remailing.

Eric Hughes · 1992-09-25 · writing
