mIRC 7.83 -- IRC Client

Installer for mIRC version 7.83, the Windows IRC (Internet Relay Chat) client that was ubiquitous in early internet culture. IRC was the primary real-time communication platform for the cypherpunks, open-source developers, hackers, and early cryptocurrency communities throughout the 1990s and 2000s. Preserved as a representative artifact of the communication infrastructure that enabled the cypherpunk movement and early digital culture.
