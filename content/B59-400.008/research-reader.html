BIP 176: Bits Denomination

Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 176 proposes “bits” as the standard term for 100 satoshis, or one millionth of a bitcoin. The informational BIP argues that a smaller whole-number denomination can reduce user error and make fiat comparisons easier. Its examples include the observation that “59 bits being $1” is easier to read than 0.000059 BTC being $1.

Jimmy Song · 2017-12-12 · writing
