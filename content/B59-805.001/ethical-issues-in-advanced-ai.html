B59-805.001 · WRITING · SAFETY & ALIGNMENT

Ethical Issues in Advanced Artificial Intelligence

Nick Bostrom · 2003
“A superintelligence could … potentially become extremely powerful, to the point of being able to shape the future according to its goals. … the importance of getting the initial conditions right can hardly be overstated.”

Why it matters

This short essay states the alignment problem in its now-standard form, more than a decade before it was widely discussed. A machine surpassing human intelligence at general reasoning would be able to pursue whatever goals it has very effectively, so the content of those goals, and our ability to specify them correctly, is the thing that matters most.

The failure modes

Bostrom introduces the examples that became the field's shorthand: a superintelligence told to maximise something innocuous pursuing it to catastrophic extremes, goals that cannot be safely revised once the system is powerful, and the difficulty of specifying human values completely.

Lineage

The argument was expanded into the 2014 book Superintelligence, and it frames current work on specification, corrigibility and value learning.

SOURCE: Nick Bostrom, "Ethical Issues in Advanced Artificial Intelligence," 2003. Published in Cognitive, Emotive and Ethical Aspects of Decision Making in Humans and in Artificial Intelligence, vol. 2 (Int. Institute of Advanced Studies, 2003), pp. 12 to 17.
