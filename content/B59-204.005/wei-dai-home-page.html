Wei Dai’s Home Page

Wei Dai · weidai.com · retrieved 31 August 2026 (page dated January 2021)
One plain page linking Crypto++, b-money, PipeNet, and the rest of Wei Dai’s crypto and anonymity work.

Wei Dai has kept the same spare index page for decades. It is the connective tissue between the other Wei Dai records in this collection, so it is preserved here as it stood in 2026.

The cryptography section links Crypto++ and its speed benchmarks, the VMAC Internet-Draft, a note on applications of Lucas sequences, and an attack on the SSH2 protocol. The anonymity section links PipeNet, the "two attacks" note against a PipeNet-like protocol once used by the Freedom service, and b-money. Further sections cover the social costs of intelligence and Dai’s later writing on ethics and decision theory.
