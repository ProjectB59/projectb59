Thexder — 1985 Developer Interview

Research record for shmuplations' translation of a 1985 BEEP magazine interview with Game Arts developers Hibiki Godai and Satoshi Uesaka about the making of Thexder. The interview covers the transforming-robot concept, PC-88 high-speed scrolling, animation, map size, data compression, working methods, and the division of design work. The page also includes a short 2022 Uesaka interview excerpt on Thexder's visual design.

Hibiki Godai & Satoshi Uesaka; translated by shmuplations · 2022-11-29 · writing
