The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks

That is the front-page headline of The Times of London for Saturday 3 January 2009 (story by Francis Elliott on Chancellor Alistair Darling and a possible second bank bailout). It fixes the block no earlier than that day and reads as a comment on the crisis Bitcoin was built in response to.
