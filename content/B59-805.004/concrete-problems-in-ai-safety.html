B59-805.004 · WRITING · SAFETY & ALIGNMENT

Concrete Problems in AI Safety

D. Amodei, C. Olah et al. · arXiv:1606.06565 · 2016
“We frame the problem of accidents in machine learning systems, defined as unintended and harmful behavior that may emerge from poor design of real-world AI systems.”

Why it matters

This paper moved AI safety from long-range argument to a near-term engineering list. It names five failure modes that show up in ordinary machine-learning systems and can be studied now, with authors from Google Brain, OpenAI and Stanford.

The five problems

Avoiding negative side effects; avoiding reward hacking (gaming the objective); scalable oversight (learning from limited human feedback); safe exploration; and robustness to distributional shift. Each is stated as a concrete research problem with possible approaches.

Lineage

It set much of the empirical safety agenda for the following decade and is a standard reference for the field's vocabulary.

SOURCE: Dario Amodei, Chris Olah, Jacob Steinhardt, Paul Christiano, John Schulman and Dan Mané, "Concrete Problems in AI Safety," arXiv:1606.06565 (2016).
