Oral History of Chip Morningstar

Computer History Museum oral history with Chip Morningstar, recorded February 4, 2019. Morningstar recounts his early computing work, Project Xanadu, Lucasfilm Games, the origins and development of Habitat, the social lessons that emerged from the world, NeoHabitat's reconstruction, AMiX, Fujitsu's Habitat work, WorldsAway, and later decentralized virtual-world systems.

Chip Morningstar, interviewed by Chris Garcia · 2019-02-04 · writing
