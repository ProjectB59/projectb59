B59-802.007 · WRITING · NEURAL NETS

ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks

A. Krizhevsky, I. Sutskever & G. E. Hinton · NeurIPS · 2012
“Our results show that a large, deep convolutional neural network is capable of achieving record-breaking results on a highly challenging dataset using purely supervised learning.”

Why it matters

AlexNet cut the top-5 error on the ImageNet benchmark from about 26 percent to about 16 percent in a single year. The margin over every hand-engineered system was so large that computer vision reorganised around deep learning within months. This is the paper usually named as the start of the modern era.

What made it work

Three things together: a big labelled dataset (ImageNet), training a large convolutional net on two GPUs, and practical choices that stuck, ReLU activations for fast training, dropout against overfitting, and heavy data augmentation.

Lineage

Everything after: VGG, GoogLeNet, ResNet, the transfer of these methods to speech and language, and the GPU-and-big-data pattern that defines the field now.

SOURCE: Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever and Geoffrey E. Hinton, "ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks," Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 25 (NeurIPS 2012).
