B59-203.002 · WRITING · DIGITAL CASH

Thoughts on Digital Cash

Eric Hughes · 1992-11-30
The whole thing hinges on the bank not being able to link the withdrawal to the deposit.

Two years before DigiCash's ecash trial, this is the list teaching itself how blind-signature cash would have to work. Hughes's focus is the property everything else depends on: the bank must not be able to connect a coin's withdrawal to its deposit. The thread is a marker of how early and how concretely the cypherpunks were thinking about payment systems that Bitcoin's designers would later inherit.
