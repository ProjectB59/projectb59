Research description

Preservation record for the open-source Habitat repository maintained by The Museum of Art and Digital Entertainment. The repository is based on the original Fujitsu technology-transfer archive and contains Commodore 64 assembly source and artwork, Stratus VOS PL/I server source, database tools, planning and beta documentation, build tooling, and multiple historical versions of Habitat data and binaries.

Citation

Museum of Art and Digital Entertainment. habitat: source and archival materials for Lucasfilm's Habitat. GitHub repository.

Provenance

The repository README states that Habitat was developed between 1985 and 1987 by Chip Morningstar and Randy Farmer at Lucasfilm, was released as Habitat in 1986 and Club Caribe in 1988, and that the source was resurrected for preservation by The MADE. The repository is based on the original Fujitsu technology-transfer archive and is licensed MIT.
