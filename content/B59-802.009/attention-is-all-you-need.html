B59-802.009 · WRITING · NEURAL NETS

Attention Is All You Need

A. Vaswani et al. · arXiv:1706.03762 · 2017
“We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely.”

Why it matters

The Transformer replaced recurrence with self-attention: every position in a sequence attends directly to every other, in one step, in parallel. Training that had been inherently sequential became a matter of large matrix multiplications, which is what modern accelerators do best. This is the architecture under GPT, BERT and essentially every large language model since.

The mechanism

Scaled dot-product attention, multiple attention heads in parallel, positional encodings to supply word order, and residual connections with layer normalisation. The design is simple enough to scale cleanly across many orders of magnitude of model size.

Lineage

Nearly the entire 2018 to present arc of language models, and increasingly of vision, audio and multimodal models, is built on this paper.

SOURCE: Ashish Vaswani et al., "Attention Is All You Need," arXiv:1706.03762 (2017); also in Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30 (2017).
