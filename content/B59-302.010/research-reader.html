John Gilmore's Home Page

Personal homepage of John Gilmore, co-founder of the Electronic Frontier Foundation, co-founder of the Cypherpunks with Eric Hughes and Timothy May, 5th employee at Sun Microsystems, and founder of Cygnus Solutions (sold to Red Hat for $675 million). The page documents his work on encryption export controls, the freedom of movement lawsuit against TSA (Gilmore v. Gonzales), the GNU Radio project, the Free Software movement, criticism of DRM, and his role hosting the original Cypherpunks Mailing List at cypherpunks@toad.com. The live site (toad.com) is currently offline; this record references the Wayback Machine capture.
